## Signature move

**The verification ledger.** A recruiter's real job on a portfolio is to check
whether the claims hold up. This page makes that literal: a slim fixed margin
rail (the "folio") accumulates a checked line each time the visitor scrolls past
a verified claim: `PL-300 — Microsoft, verified`, `750 hrs/yr saved —
FrieslandCampina`, `58,000+ tickets — 6 validated use cases`, and so on. Each
entry stamps in with a check mark and stays. By the colophon the rail is a
complete, ticked record of everything the page claimed, doubling as the proof
that the person is real. Coded in the page off `--sc-p` and scroll position,
not an engine device. Only real, verifiable figures appear in it (the honesty
check on the move). Gated to pointer/fine + reduced-motion safe; on mobile it
collapses to a single running tally line.
