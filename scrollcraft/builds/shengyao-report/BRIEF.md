- **Unit:** the exhibit, meaning one claim, one chart drawn from his real figures, and a caption with its source. No prose spread longer than two short paragraphs.

- **Hero:** the report cover. Layered portrait cutout with his name set behind him.
- **Close:** sign-off page with the email set as the headline, plus copy, LinkedIn and CV.
